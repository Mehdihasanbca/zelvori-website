GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can AI automate every business process?

No. Processes with ambiguous rules, high consequences or unreliable source data may need human review or conventional software instead.

Can automation connect to our existing tools?

It depends on APIs, permissions and vendor limits. We assess available integration paths and security requirements during discovery.

How do you measure whether automation helps?

Choose practical measures such as handling time, error rates, review effort and successful completion before and after the workflow changes.
