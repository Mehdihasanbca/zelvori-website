GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Should my business choose a CRM, HRMS or ERP?

Start with the workflow causing the most friction. Requirements, team size, integrations and reporting needs help determine the right scope.

Can you customize software for our existing process?

We can map your current process and identify what should remain, change or integrate before defining a solution.

Can you migrate data from a spreadsheet?

It may be possible after reviewing data formats, quality, duplicates, permissions and the target system's import capabilities.
