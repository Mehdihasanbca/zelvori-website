## 3. Key Findings & Strategic Insights

1. **The "Theme Trap" vs "Engineering Quality":** Cheap low-end providers (₹5k–₹15k) rely heavily on fragile WordPress plugins, stolen themes, and unoptimized page builders that suffer severe performance and security deficits. Zelvori delivers engineered Next.js/TypeScript codebases with verified security, zero bloat, and full IP handover.
2. **Transparent Line-Item Scoping:** Most agencies obscure scope to demand aggressive change-order fees mid-project. Zelvori's competitive moat is upfront clarity: explicit page/screen/module caps, transparent third-party pass-through costs (hosting, domain, tokens), and milestone sign-offs.
3. **No Unsubstantiated "Cheapest" Claims:** Zelvori intentionally does not position itself as the cheapest provider in India. We position ourselves as the highest-discipline, most transparent software engineering partner for ambitious businesses.
